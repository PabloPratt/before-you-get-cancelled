⚡ No archive? Scan straight from X

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: 🧹 Scan my X profile
  2. On x.com, open your own profile → Replies tab (or Likes), then click the bookmark.
  3. It scrolls your profile, saves a file, and you drop that file above.

Free, no login to this site, nothing uploaded. It only runs on the profile you're logged in as. X only loads about your most recent 3,200 posts this way; the archive has everything.
